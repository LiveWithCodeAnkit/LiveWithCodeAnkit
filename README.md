## About me

I'm a **Full Stack & AI Engineer** based in Surat, India, with 3+ years of end-to-end ownership on production systems for the UAE insurance market. I take products from requirements to production: React and Next.js frontends, Node.js and FastAPI backends, MongoDB, PostgreSQL and Oracle, and LLM systems that call real tools instead of guessing.

- 🤖 Built and shipped, solo, a **WhatsApp AI contact center** that handles **2,000 to 5,000 chats a day**
- 📄 Designed a **document AI API** that reads **30+ document types** into structured JSON at **97% accuracy**
- ⚡ Cut MongoDB report queries from **20–60 s to under 100 ms**
- 🏢 Built **10+ FastAPI services on Oracle** for policy issuance and claims settlement, and claim flows in a **six-portal insurance CRM**
- 🛠️ AI-native every day with Cursor and Claude Code
- 🎯 Open to **Full Stack, Applied AI, and LLM engineering** roles, remote or Delhi NCR
- ⚡ Fun fact: I speak 5 languages: English, Hindi, Haryanvi, Gujarati, and Punjabi
